# Timbum: architecture

Timbum is a blocky FPS built with Godot 4.7 (GDScript, Forward+, Jolt): free-for-all deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Gun Game, King of the Block, Infected, or 25 v 25 Tactical (`Net.config.mode`).
Matches mix bots and humans. Humans join a host over ENet (LAN discovery, room code, IP), Steam or WebRTC.

## Design decisions

These were settled up front, choosing the standard option each time:

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Mode | Free-for-all deathmatch. First to the kill limit (default 20) or the most kills when time runs out (default 8 min). After a match: every peer's camera cuts to the winner (`World._start_win_cam`), whose avatar plays their win emote (`peer_info.emote`) for `World.WIN_SHOW` (4 s), then the results screen (final table, match highlights for best streak / most headshots / fewest deaths, an itemised coins/XP breakdown, and the next map/mutator/time of day, which the server decides at match end with `Net.plan_next_match()` and sends in `_s_match_end`), then that match `World.RESULTS_TIME` (16 s, including the emote) after the match ended, or as soon as a majority of the session's humans vote to skip (`World.vote_skip()`); the server broadcasts the vote count (`_s_skip_votes`, also sent to a joiner arriving mid-results) and every peer's results screen shows "N of M ready (K needed)" with M and K taken from `Net.players`. The Tab scoreboard stays available over the results screen (`Hud.show_results` puts it on top). The results screen also has primary/secondary pickers; a change is saved to the profile and sent with `Net.update_my_info()`, so it applies from the first spawn of the next match. |
| Mode controllers | Every mode's rules live in one node: `World.rules`, a `MatchMode` (`scripts/game/match_mode.gd`) that `MatchMode.create()` picks from `Net.config.mode` on every peer and World adds as its child (named after the mode, so the mode's own RPCs reach the same node everywhere; World creates it before the Hud, whose scoreboard asks it for columns, and adds it to the tree after). World, Hud and BotBrain never test the mode; they call the controller's hooks: team for a newcomer, the info a player spawns with, spawn point, spawn protection, respawn delay (negative: none), whether the clock runs and attacks are blocked, bot balance, joins and leaves, what a kill does, which weapon the server accepts a hit from (`weapon_allowed`), the end of the match (`server_finish(rows)` orders the final rows and returns what the results need, e.g. `{winner}`), whether a next match follows on a timer, the HUD's top bars and lines, the free-for-all race (`race_value` / `race_limit` / `race_text`: kills, Gun Game level or King of the Block points), compass pips, the scoreboard's stat columns (`score_columns` / `row_cells`), grouping and tags, the results title, banner and extra highlights, and bot goals (`bot_roam_goal`, `bot_min_range`, `bot_hold_area`). The base class is plain Deathmatch; `TeamDeathmatch`, `GunGame`, `KingOfTheBlock`, `Infected` and `Tactical` subclass it. A new mode adds a `Data.MODES` entry, a subclass and a case in `MatchMode.create()`; `Net.config.kill_limit` is each mode's limit (`Net.sanitize_config` sets the mode's default). Team rules every team mode shares are in `Teams` (`scripts/game/teams.gd`): `Teams.is_team_mode()` (`Data.MODES[mode].teams`; team-only features ask this), `Player.info.team` (0 / 1, set by the server at spawn, -1 in free-for-all), names and colours (a mode may rename its sides, e.g. Infected's SURVIVORS / INFECTED), `Teams.pick` (a human joins the side with fewer humans, a bot the side with fewer players), armbands and nametags (teammates' tags through walls). `World.is_enemy` is everyone else except teammates; `World._server_damage` ignores hits between teammates in every team mode (your own grenade still hurts you). |
| Team Deathmatch | `"tdm"`, `TeamDeathmatch` (`scripts/game/team_deathmatch.gd`). Deathmatch maps, respawns and mutators as in Deathmatch. Each kill of an enemy adds 1 to the killer's team score (`TeamDeathmatch.score`, kept by the server so a leaver's kills still count, sent with `_s_score(alpha, bravo)`, also to joiners). The first team to `Net.config.kill_limit` wins (setup offers 25-100; `Net.sanitize_config` defaults it to `DEFAULT_KILL_LIMIT` 50 for this mode), otherwise the higher score at the time limit; equal is a draw (`winner` -1). Bots: `World.server_balance_bots` adds each bot to the smaller team and, when a human joins, removes one from the bigger team (`MatchMode.bot_drop_team`). Spawns rank the map's spawns by distance to the nearest living enemy (capped at 45 m), +10 on the team's half of the map (the longer axis; ALPHA the low end), +8 with a teammate within 20 m, and rule out spots an enemy can see or within 8 m of one; the pick varies among the best three within 6 points. Results rows carry `team`, `alive` and `won` (the winning team first); the title shows the score. |
| Gun Game | `"gungame"`, `GunGame` (`scripts/game/gun_game.gd`), free-for-all on the deathmatch maps with respawns. One ladder for everyone (`GunGame.steps`): the guns of `FULL_LADDER` (SMG, AR-Cube, Tri-Cube, LMG, shotgun, DMR, sniper, rocket, Bitstorm MP, sawn-off, Tri-Tap, revolver, Whisper, Block 9), then the knife. `Net.config.kill_limit` is the ladder length: setup offers 6, 10 or 15 steps (`LENGTHS`; default and maximum `max_length()` = 15); a shorter ladder keeps the first and last gun and spreads the rest evenly (`GunGame.ladder`). A kill of another player moves the killer up one step; a knife kill (also the quick melee) moves the victim down one; suicides and falls change nothing. The first knife kill on the last step ends the match; at the time limit the highest step wins (then kills, then fewest deaths). The step is `Player.info.ladder`; `step_info` builds the info for it (the step's gun in its own slot, the other slot empty, both empty on the knife step, `no_grenades`). The server sends `_s_step(eid, step, by)`; every peer applies it with `Player.apply_info`, and the promoted player (local or bot) draws the new gun with `Player.switch_to`, so the draw animation plays and the gun can't fire before `switch_time()`. Late joiners get the step inside each entity's info. Grenades are off (frags and tacticals: `info.no_grenades`, see `Player.carries_grenades`; the server's per-life counts are 0 and grenade crates skip them), so no kill skips the ladder; loadouts and loadout mutators (Rocket Party, Snipers Only) are ignored and never rolled. Hit validation (`GunGame.weapon_allowed`) takes the knife, the step's gun and, for `SWAP_GRACE` (0.6 s) after a promotion, the gun it replaced (shots and rockets already in flight). HUD: a ladder card (`GunGameCard`, `ui/gun_game_card.gd`, top left: LEVEL x / N, the gun now and next, a pip per step), the race on level, and announcements for promotions, setbacks and whoever reaches the knife. The scoreboard shows LEVEL / KILLS / DEATHS sorted by level; the results add "won it with the knife" and the most knife setbacks. Bots use whatever gun their step gives them and, on the knife step, hunt the nearest player (`bot_roam_goal`). Rewards: the first row (the knife winner, or the highest level at time-up) is `won`. |
| King of the Block | `"koth"`, `KingOfTheBlock` (`scripts/game/king_of_the_block.gd`), free-for-all on the deathmatch maps with respawns. Zone spots (`KingOfTheBlock.zone_spots`) are the map's health/ammo/grenade pickup spots, at least 10 m apart (5-9 per map), all with a navmesh path from every spawn (checked on all 8 maps). The zone is a 4 m-radius cylinder from 1 m below to 3.5 m above its floor. The server alone decides: one living player alone in it holds it and scores a point per full second held; two or more contest it and nobody scores; the hold progress restarts whenever the holder changes. Every `MOVE_INTERVAL` (60 s) of match clock the zone moves to a random other spot; `WARNING` (10 s) before, every peer announces it and shows the next spot faintly. `Net.config.kill_limit` is the points to win (setup offers 60-200, default `DEFAULT_SCORE_LIMIT` 120); otherwise the most points at the time limit win (then kills). RPCs: `_s_zone(index, next, move_at)` (`move_at` is the `World.time_left` of the next move, so every peer counts down from the synced clock), `_s_hold(holder, contested, progress)`, `_s_point(eid, total, progress)`, and `_s_state(...)` with all of it for joiners. The zone is drawn on every peer as an additive glowing ring, floor disc and tall beam, coloured free (gold) / yours (green) / someone else's (red) / contested (pulsing magenta). `ZoneOverlay` (`ui/zone_overlay.gd`) draws the zone marker with its distance (on the screen edge when off screen, plus the next spot during the warning) and a card: holder, CONTESTED, the capture progress bar, your points and the move countdown; the compass gets a pip (`compass_pips`). Scoreboard: POINTS / KILLS / DEATHS by points; the results add the longest hold. Bots head for their own point inside the zone (`bot_roam_goal`), and while it exists (`bot_hold_area`) they fight on their way in, never chase out of it, and only close in on targets inside it. Rewards: the first row (most points) is `won`. |
| Infected | `"infected"`, `Infected` (`scripts/game/infected.gd`). Everyone starts a Survivor (team 0). `START_DELAY` (10 s) into the match the server turns a random living player Infected (`_s_role(eid, role, first)`); a Survivor who dies for any reason turns Infected at the kill (after the kill feed shows them as a Survivor) and respawns as one. An Infected's info (`Infected.infect_info`) has team 1, empty primary/secondary, `knife_only` (no grenades or tactical: the server's per-life counts are 0 and grenade crates skip them), `max_hp` 150 and `speed` 1.15; `Player.apply_info` rebuilds slots and speed on every peer, and the avatar gets a green `Avatar.set_tint`. The server ends the match when no Survivor is left (Infected win) or at the time limit (Survivors win); if every Infected leaves, a new one is picked. Joiners (and new bots) after the start are Infected; a human joining pushes out an Infected bot first, never the last of a side. Loadout mutators (Rocket Party, Snipers Only) are never rolled or accepted. Rows carry `team`, `won` (the winning side) and `infections`; the results list the survivors and the top infector instead of "fewest deaths". Infected bots always head for the nearest Survivor (`bot_roam_goal`); Survivor bots keep 9 m away from an Infected (`bot_min_range`). |
| Tactical mode | `Net.config.mode == "tactical"` (`Data.MODES`). Always `Data.TACTICAL_MAP` (Block HQ), 50 players (`Tactical.PLAYERS`) and no mutators; `Net.sanitize_config` forces these. Its rules are the `Tactical` mode controller (`scripts/game/tactical.gd`, a `MatchMode`, World's child "Tactical"; `World.tactical` points to it for its own UI): teams, spawns, bot balance, kills and the end of the match. **Teams:** `Player.info.team` (0 ALPHA west, 1 BRAVO east), set by the server at spawn (`Teams`). A human joins the side with fewer humans; bots fill each side to 25 (`TEAM_SIZE`). Bots are only added during planning: a human arriving later takes over a living bot of their side (position, health, weapons) or spectates if none is left; a human who leaves alive is replaced by a bot the same way. **Phases:** PLANNING (30 s, `PLANNING_TIME`): translucent barriers (a `StaticBody3D` on layer 1 over `building_map.gd` `BARRIERS`) close each staging room, `World.attacks_blocked()` stops firing, knifing and throwing (client and server) and the match clock does not run; then FIGHT (`_s_phase`), then OVER. **One life:** no respawns and no spawn protection; `World._server_damage` ignores hits between teammates (your own grenade still hurts you). The match ends when a side has nobody alive (`Tactical.server_check_end`, after every kill and leave) or at the time limit (more alive wins; equal is a draw). `_s_match_end` rows then also carry `team`, `alive` and `won` (the winning side first, survivors before the fallen), `next` is `{winner}` (0, 1, -1 for a draw) and `wait` is `WIN_SHOW + 2`: every camera watches the winning side's top survivor perform their win emote under a "<TEAM> WINS!" banner (skipped on a draw), then the results screen shows. Nothing advances on a timer: its NEW GAME button (host or offline only; clients wait or leave) calls `Net.next_match()`. A team win counts as victory (`Profile.record_match` placement 1), a draw pays like second place. **Found weapons:** slots start empty (`""`). Weapon spots are the map's pickups of kind `"weapon"` plus the gun each dead player drops; each holds one weapon id the server rolls (`WEAPON_WEIGHTS`: launchers and snipers rare). Standing on a spot with that slot empty takes it (bots also swap up by `BOT_RATING`); the `interact` key swaps it for the gun in that slot (`_c_interact`). `_s_give(eid, slot, wid)` updates `Player.slots` and `info` on every peer, so the server's hit check (weapon in the shooter's `Player.slots`) accepts picked-up guns and rejects any other; `_s_spot` updates the spot. A picked-up gun comes with a full magazine (`Player.mag_size`, so Extended Mag applies) and the standard reserve. **Planning tools:** the floor plan (`TacticalMap`, `tactical_map` key, opened automatically at the start) shows each storey with living teammates and weapon spots; clicks place ATTACK and HOLD markers (`_c_marker`, at most 3 per player, the oldest replaced). The server sends each peer only its own team's markers (`_s_markers`); the HUD draws them in the world (`TacticalOverlay`) and on the compass (`Hud.Compass.pips`). **Bot squads:** when planning ends the server groups each side's bots into squads of 5, each on a different shuffled route from `building_map.gd` `ROUTES` (continued through the mirrored east half into the enemy staging room). A squad moves on from a waypoint once 60% of its armed members are within 6 m and it has held there 3-6 s (longer after contact; never more than 25 s on one waypoint), crouching while it holds and spread across the objective. ATTACK markers pull the two nearest free squads (sprinting), HOLD markers one (digging in there). With 4 or fewer enemies left, or 3 minutes in, squads hunt the nearest enemy. For 35 s after the barriers drop an unarmed bot first heads for the nearest unclaimed weapon spot (`_claim_spot`). `BotBrain` rereads its order every 0.3 s (`Tactical.bot_order`), targets only enemies (`World.is_enemy`) and still chases a fresh sighting within 18 m. **Pacing:** no grapple hook and no sliding (Player and BotBrain skip both when `world.tactical` is set). **Spectating:** 3 s after death the local view moves to `Tactical`'s SpectateCam, over the shoulder of a living teammate (anyone alive once the team is gone); fire/aim cycle. Teams show as coloured armbands on both arms, team-coloured nametags (teammates' through walls) and kill feed names, and living-player bars per team in the HUD's top panel. **Performance:** on this Apple Silicon Mac (8 GB), a 50-player offline match at the default (Medium) preset, windowed 1280x720 with vsync off, averaged 108-117 FPS (display-capped near 120) with 1% lows of 36-49 FPS in the heaviest firefights; headless (simulation only) it runs at about 145 FPS. Weapon spots stop drawing past 32 m and bots read squad orders at 3 Hz; no further think-rate LOD was needed. |
| Players | 2-12 per match. Bots fill up to the target player count (default 8). When a human joins, one bot leaves (a dead bot if possible, otherwise one nobody is fighting, then the lowest scorer); when a human leaves, a bot comes back. Respawns rank spawns by distance to the nearest living player, with any spawn a living player can see ranked below every hidden one; the pick varies among the best three, but only between spawns as hidden as the best one and at least 5 m from any living player. |
| Networking | ENet, default port **7777**. The host is the server and also plays. Clients run their own movement and hit detection (trusted, no server-side line-of-sight check). The server owns health, kills, score, pickups, bots, projectiles, and the match clock. It sanity-checks every hit request: shooter and target alive, weapon allowed by the mode (`MatchMode.weapon_allowed`: the knife or a gun in the shooter's current slots, `Player.slots`: the loadout, in Tactical whatever they picked up, in Gun Game the step's gun) and not a projectile weapon (rockets only deal damage through the server's own projectile), a fire-rate budget per shooter shared by all guns (each shot costs its weapon's refire interval, with slack for 3 bunched shots; the knife has its own), total damage per target capped at one shot's maximum, and knife range. Spawning grants 2 s of protection from other players' damage (`World.SPAWN_PROTECTION`); firing a shot, rocket or grenade ends your own protection early. The server announces it with `_s_protection(eid, seconds)`, which every peer shows as a pulsing cyan shell on the avatar (`Avatar.set_protected`) and the protected player sees as a "SPAWN PROTECTED" countdown above the crosshair. Offline play uses `OfflineMultiplayerPeer`, so the same code runs everywhere. `Net.PROTOCOL_VERSION` must match between host and client; the host kicks mismatched clients. |
| Connections | `Net.host(port, cfg, link)` / `Net.join(address, port)` / `Net.join_code(code)` / `Net.join_steam_lobby(id)`, built on `addons/netlink`. `Link.DIRECT` is one ENet server reachable four ways: `NetLinkLan` answers UDP discovery queries on port 47777 (Join lists the host), `NetLinkNoray` registers the ENet socket with the public noray server (`tomfol.io:8890`) for a room code (the noray open id; NAT punch-through first, relay on failure, and `Net` retries a failed direct link through the relay), and addresses (LAN, Tailscale, ZeroTier, hostnames) still work. `Link.STEAM` uses a Steam lobby (Spacewar app 480) and GodotSteam's `SteamMultiplayerPeer` (`addons/godotsteam`); the lobby id is the code and overlay invites call `Net.join_steam_lobby`. `Link.WEBRTC` uses `WebRTCMultiplayerPeer` (`addons/webrtc`, webrtc-native) with PeerJS signalling and Google STUN; codes are `W-` plus 6 letters. Every MultiplayerPeer is server-relayed, so gameplay code never knows which link is in use. The host's pause menu shows how to join (`Net.room_code`, `Net.invite_changed`). |
| Art | All art is procedural; there are no external assets. Voxel maps use 1 m blocks, a 16x16 pixel-art texture atlas, vertex ambient occlusion, and ramps for elevation changes. The block shader (`BlockAtlas._SHADER_CODE`) bends normals by each texel's brightness (pixel-art relief, fading out past 35 m) and reads per-tile surface properties from the `layer_props` table (`BlockAtlas._SURFACES`: smoothness, metallic, foliage sway, water ripple); its `detail` uniform (the `block_detail` setting, `BlockAtlas.set_detail`) switches the relief, sway and ripples off. `PostProcess` (a canvas layer under the HUD) sharpens, grades per time of day and vignettes; the `post_fx` setting hides it. Video settings apply live through `Profile._apply_graphics`: `anti_aliasing` (off, FXAA, MSAA 2x/4x, TAA) and `render_scale` on the root viewport, and `shadows`, `ambient_occlusion`, `bloom` and `reflections` on every map sun and `WorldEnvironment` in the `MapBuilder.GRAPHICS_GROUP` group (`MapBuilder.apply_graphics`). The `quality` preset sets view distance, shadow detail and SSIL when a map is built, and picking it in Settings switches those toggles to `Profile.QUALITY_PRESETS`. Characters, guns, and hats are built from boxes. |
| Audio | All sound effects and the chiptune music are synthesised at startup into `AudioStreamWAV`. |
| Variety | Map rotation is shuffled each cycle. Each match the server rolls a time of day (day/sunset/night) and, when mutators are on, a mutator half the time (`Data.MUTATORS`: low gravity, turbo, big heads, vampire, rocket party, snipers only, glass cannon). Both travel to clients inside `Net.config`. Loadout mutators are applied by the server at spawn (`World._apply_loadout_mutator`): the mutator's gun (`World.LOADOUT_MUTATORS`: rocket for Rocket Party, sniper for Snipers Only) becomes the primary and the player's own primary moves into the secondary slot (a player whose primary already is that gun keeps the sidearm); Rocket Party also doubles the rocket's reserve and crate refill (`Player._ammo_scale`). Bots holding a sniper or rocket switch to their secondary (unless it is a long gun too) inside `BotBrain.LONG_GUN_CLOSE` (12 m / 9 m) and back beyond it plus `LONG_GUN_SLACK`. |
| Movement | Slide: crouch pressed while sprinting on the ground above `Player.SLIDE_MIN_ENTRY` starts a slide at `SLIDE_SPEED` (or current speed + `SLIDE_BOOST`) that bleeds `SLIDE_FRICTION`, gains speed downhill, steers a little and ends on release, a wall, low speed or `SLIDE_MAX_TIME`; jumping keeps its speed. Peek: `lean` (-1..1) moves the eye `LEAN_DIST` sideways and rolls the camera, clamped by a sphere cast so the eye never enters a wall; head and body hitboxes follow, and remotes get the lean quantised into state-flag bits 16-19 (`FLAG_SLIDE` carries the slide). Prone: the "prone" action toggles `Player.prone` (on the ground, not grappling; a slide can dive into it). The collider shrinks to `PRONE_HEIGHT`, the eye drops to `PRONE_EYE`, speed is `PRONE_SPEED`, spread x0.6 and recoil x0.65; the body hitbox lies flat (`PRONE_BODY`) reaching back along the legs. Crouch, jump or prone again gets up into a crouch if there is headroom (the jump press does not also jump). No sprint, peek, slide or grapple while prone. Remotes get it as `FLAG_PRONE`; the avatar tips face down (`Avatar.PRONE_LIFT`/`PRONE_BACK`) with head and aim tilted back up. Grapple: a world ray up to `GRAPPLE_RANGE` (never the invisible `Bounds`) anchors a rope that pulls the player in with damped swing until close, timed out, blocked or released; the anchor reaches other peers through `World.report_grapple` -> `_c_grapple` -> `_s_grapple`. Movement stays client-trusted like the rest. |
| Pause | In an offline (vs bots) match the pause menu freezes the match: `World.set_frozen` pauses the scene tree (and physics) and stops `World.now()`, the match clock every timer reads (respawns, pickups, smoke, flashes, spawn protection), so nothing jumps ahead on resume. The pause menu runs with `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`. Online matches keep running. |
| Grenades | Frags (`Data.GRENADE`) explode on first contact with the world or a hitbox (`Projectile._step_grenade`); `fuse` only caps a throw that hits nothing. Tacticals (`Data.TACTICALS`, one per life, chosen in the locker and sent as `peer_info.tactical`) bounce until their fuse. The server validates the kind against the thrower's loadout and its per-life count, and deals no damage for them; `_s_explode(pid, pos, kind)` makes every peer add the smoke cloud to `World.smokes` (bots can't see through it: `World.smoke_blocks`) or blind players by `World.flash_amount` (distance, line of sight and facing): the HUD whites out for the local player, bots lose sight until `Player.flashed_until`. |
| Killstreaks | `Streaks` (`scripts/game/streaks.gd`), World's child "Streaks" on every peer, active when `Net.config.streaks` is on (match setup's KILLSTREAKS row; `Net.sanitize_config` defaults it per mode with `Streaks.default_for`: on in Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch and King of the Block, off in Gun Game, Infected and Tactical). The streak is the server's `World.scores[eid].streak` (kills without dying); a kill with the reward's weapon id `Streaks.WEAPON` (`"bomber"`) counts as a kill but `World._server_kill` doesn't extend the streak (nor `best_streak`, nor the local streak callouts). After the mode's `server_on_kill`, `Streaks.server_on_kill` grants the reward the killer (alive) just reached with `_s_reward(eid, kind, streak)`: 3 `radar` (every peer keeps `radar_until[eid]`; for 8 s the owner, and in team modes their team, get every living enemy as a compass pip and an on-screen marker; enemies of the owner see ENEMY RADAR), 5 `shield` (`Player.shield` = 50 on every peer) and 7 `bomber` (`bomber_ready[eid]`). **Overshield:** `World._server_damage` takes damage (after Glass Cannon doubles it) from the shield first, then health, and sends the shield left as the fifth argument of `_s_damaged`; hit confirms, damage numbers and the death screen's damage total count both, and its killer line adds the killer's shield. Vampire, regeneration and health crates refill health only (to `max_health()`, 150 for the Infected), never the shield. **Block Bomber:** the `streak_reward` key (4) opens targeting (`Streaks.targeting`: the strike line previewed on the ground; `Player._read_input` gates fire meanwhile), fire or the key calls it on the aim point (`aim_point`, world ray up to 120 m; aim cancels); `request_bomber` → `_c_call(point)` → the server checks holder, alive, range, `attacks_blocked`, computes 5 points 4 m apart along the caller→aim direction centred on the aim point, each dropped onto what is below it (`strike_points`; a roof shelters the floor), and broadcasts `_s_strike(id, caller, points, delay, done)` (2 s red ground marks with a light beam on every peer, siren, an AIRSTRIKE countdown label and a compass pip). From `land_at` the server sends `_s_blast(id, index, pos)` every 0.16 s (explosion effect and sound) and deals `World.server_blast(pos, 5 m, 140, caller, "bomber", self_scale 0)`: credited to the caller even if they died meanwhile, never hurting the caller, no friendly fire (in Gun Game, where killstreaks are only on when switched on, a bomber kill promotes like any kill that isn't the knife's). Death (`on_death`, from `_s_killed` and `_s_despawn` on every peer) clears that player's radar, shield and unused bomber (a called strike still lands); `on_match_over` clears everything. Late joiners get `_s_state(radar seconds left, shields, bomber holders, strikes)`. Bots: `bot_consider` calls a held bomber 0.8-2.5 s after earning it on a visible target 14-120 m away with no teammate near it; `bot_radar_goal` sends a bot with radar to the nearest enemy; under a warning a bot runs (`BotBrain.DODGE_CHANCE` 30/55/80 % per strike, reacting `DODGE_REACTION` 1.1/0.8/0.5 s after the call). UI: `StreakOverlay` (`ui/streak_overlay.gd`, canvas layer 11), the shield segments on the Hud's health card, `Hud.BomberIcon` in the kill feed. |
| Quick ping | `Pings` (`scripts/game/pings.gd`), World's child "Pings" on every peer, active only in team modes (`Pings.active()` = `Teams.is_team_mode()`); elsewhere it doesn't read input and the server ignores pings. The `ping` key (middle mouse) casts the aim ray (world and hitboxes, 150 m): an enemy hitbox makes an enemy ping, anything else a spot ping (`ping_from_aim`); `request` → `_c_ping(pos, target)`. The server (`_server_ping`) checks the pinger is alive, the spot within range and the rate limit (`MIN_GAP` 0.6 s apart, `BURST` 3 per `BURST_WINDOW` 5 s; rejected pings are dropped silently), snaps an enemy ping to that enemy's position (an enemy that died or isn't an enemy makes it a spot ping) and sends `_s_ping(owner, pos, target)` only to the human teammates' peers (and runs it locally when the host is one); `relayed(owner, pos, target, recipients)` reports who got it. Receivers keep one ping per owner for `LIFE` (6 s): `PingOverlay` (`ui/ping_overlay.gd`, canvas layer 11) draws a red triangle (ENEMY) or a gold pin with the pinger's name and the distance, clamped to the screen edge, fading in its last second; `compass_pips` adds pips; the sound is `ping` / `ping_enemy`. Bots: on a new target `BotBrain._perceive` calls `bot_spotted`, which pings that enemy unless this bot pinged in the last `BOT_GAP` (8 s) or its team pinged that enemy in the last `BOT_REPEAT` (4 s); a teammate bot without a target within `BOT_HEED_RANGE` (40 m) of an enemy ping searches there (`BotBrain.heed_ping`). Pings aren't sent to late joiners (they last seconds). |
| Input | `Profile.DEFAULT_BINDINGS` plus the player's overrides in `Profile.bindings` (saved in the profile) build the InputMap. Codes are `k<physical keycode>` or `m<mouse button>`; binding a code removes it from any other action, and Esc is reserved for the menus. Settings > Controls captures new bindings; on-screen hints read `Profile.key_text(action)`. |
| Maps | Village, Desert Ruins, Factory, Sky Islands, Snow Fort, Harbor Docks, Jungle Temple, City Rooftops (`Data.MAP_ROTATION`, the deathmatch maps), and Block HQ (`building`), which only Tactical plays. Each is generated deterministically from code, so every peer builds identical geometry from the map id. Water (`BlockAtlas.WATER`) is a solid block placed below the map's `kill_y`, so falling in kills like the sky map's void. Block HQ (`scripts/world/maps/building_map.gd`) is 112 x 64 m: three storeys walking at y 1, 6 and 11 (`FLOORS`), the east half mirroring the west so both teams get the same building. Each team's two-storey staging room has five exits plus two doors to the alleys; between them a lattice of rooms with doors and one-block-high shooting windows, a central ground-floor hallway, an open office on the first floor and an open-plan top floor, a north and a south switchback stairwell to the top floor and a centre flight per storey on each side, a three-storey atrium with balconies in the middle, and alleys all round the outside. Its layout constants (`SPAWN_ROOM`, `BARRIERS`, `ROUTES`, `WEAPON_SPOTS`, ...) are in grid space; Tactical mirrors x (`SIZE.x - 1 - x`) for team BRAVO. Every spawn, pickup, barrier opening and route waypoint was checked reachable on the navmesh from team ALPHA's staging room. |
| Economy | Coins come from kills, headshots, placement, level-ups, and a 24-hour daily reward. The shop sells guns, weapon skins (one equipped skin applies to every gun), hats, outfits, gun charms, win emotes and draw animations, plus attachment upgrades (`Data.ATTACHMENTS`): levels bought one at a time, applying to every gun, each costing `ATTACHMENT_GROWTH` (1.75) times the last. Nothing is random; there are no loot boxes. Everything persists in `user://profile.json`, and Stats can export it to / import it from any file. |
| Attachments | A player's levels travel in `peer_info.mods` (sanitized by `Net.sanitize_info`, clamped to each max) and become stat multipliers in `Player.setup` (`Player.mod(id)` = `Data.attachment_mult`). They only touch client-side handling: hip spread, recoil, bloom, magazine size (`Player.mag_size`), reload (`Player.reload_time`), aim-in time, falloff range (`Player.damage_at(w, dist, range_mult)`), aiming move speed, switch time (`Player.switch_time`) and grapple cooldown. Damage per shot and fire rate never change, so the server's hit validation (per-shot damage cap, fire-rate budget) is unchanged. Bots have no upgrades. Laser Sight level 1+ adds a `LaserSight` beam on that player's gun, seen by everyone. |
| Cosmetics | Charms (`GunCharm`) hang from `Models.charm_anchor()` on every gun but the knife and swing as a Verlet pendulum under world gravity, so turning, recoil and draws move them. Draw animations are `Viewmodel.draw_pose(style, d)`; the draw always lasts exactly `Player.switch_time()`, so no style changes when the gun can fire. |
| Per-weapon stats | Counted for the local player only, on its own peer, in every mode (`World.my_weapon_stats`: id -> `{kills, headshots, shots, hits, damage}`), and added to `Profile.weapon_stats` by `Profile.record_match` at the match end. Ids are `Data.WEAPONS` plus `Data.STAT_EXTRA_SOURCES` (`grenade`, `bomber`). A **shot** is one round leaving the gun, counted when it is fired (`World.report_shot` for hitscan rounds and knife swings, `World.request_projectile` for rockets): one trigger pull of a single-shot gun, each round of a burst (a 3-round burst is 3 shots), one shotgun shell whatever its pellets do, one rocket, one knife swing. A **hit** is a shot that struck at least one enemy, at most one per shot, so accuracy is 0-100 %: hitscan and knife hits are decided client-side when the shot is fired (any hitbox of a living non-teammate in the shot's hit list); a rocket counts as a hit once when the server confirms its blast hurt someone (the first `_s_hit_confirm` with `wid == "rocket"` in a frame). **Damage** is the server's `dealt` from `_s_hit_confirm(head, killed, amount, pos, wid)` (health plus Overshield taken). **Kills / headshots** come from `_s_killed` when this peer is the killer, credited to the kill's `wid`, so frag and Block Bomber kills go to `grenade` / `bomber`, never to the held gun; falls are not counted. The results screen shows the match's accuracy (all hits / all shots) and most-used weapon (most shots, ties to more kills) from `record_match`'s answer (`World.match_rewards`). |
| Bots | Run on the server and use the Godot navigation mesh, baked at runtime from the map. Easy/Normal/Hard set reaction time, aim error (a floor of error never goes away), turn speed, view range and trigger discipline. The aim error wanders: it drifts smoothly toward a new random miss every 0.25-0.6 s (and eases between chest and head), so a bot's crosshair sways around you rather than twitching. Bots also have to read your movement: their aim trails the part of your sideways velocity they haven't caught up with yet (`BotBrain.READ_RATE`/`READ_ACCURACY`/`TRACK_LAG`), so standing still costs nothing, steady strafing helps a little, and changing direction throws their aim off until they re-read you. Getting shot makes a bot flinch (its error jumps, then settles over 0.5 s), and melee/grenades also wait for the reaction delay. Grenades: with a target in range a bot considers a throw every 2.5-5 s (`THROW_CHECK`) and takes it with a per-difficulty chance (`FRAG_CHANCE`, `TACTICAL_CHANCE`), then rests 8-14 s; frags go at targets 7-24 m away or at the last-seen spot of one that ducked out of sight 0.4-3 s ago, flashes at targets 6-22 m away, smokes only when the bot is at 70 HP or less. The aim solves the real lob (`_throw_aim`, throw speed plus the 3 m/s upward kick, gravity, recoil) and a throw is skipped if a wall is within 3 m in front. Target choice prefers close, weak rivals and whoever is shooting the bot, leans modestly toward visible players ahead of it on kills (up to 7.5 m "closer"), and away from players other bots are already fighting (5 m per bot), so the leader gets hunted without the whole lobby piling on. A subtle catch-up rule scales aim error (and, halved, reaction time) by up to ±20% per target: bots tighten up against whoever is far ahead of them on kills or on a 3+ streak, and ease off against players trailing them. Each bot rolls an aggression personality (cautious lurkers vs hunters). Bots loot pickups when hurt, strafe, jump, crouch, and use every gun and the knife. They use the movement mechanics through the same inputs as players, each with a per-difficulty chance: they slide on sprints with a clear run ahead (`SLIDE_CHANCE`), peek out sideways in ranged fights (`PEEK_CHANCE`), go prone to snipe targets past 20 m and get up when one closes within 12 m or hurts them (`PRONE_CHANCE`), and grapple up ledges at least 2.5 m higher on their route, across gaps the navmesh can't bridge, or (aggressive bots) toward distant targets (`GRAPPLE_CHANCE`; the hook point must be a world surface the ray reaches). |

## Rendering the viewmodel

Everything uses visual layer 1. The first-person arms and gun (`scripts/game/viewmodel.gd`) swap their materials for a shader that squashes depth toward the near plane, so the gun draws over walls instead of clipping into them while still being lit by the scene.

The local player's own avatar uses `cast_shadow = SHADOW_CASTING_SETTING_SHADOWS_ONLY`.

## Contracts between modules

### Data (autoload, `scripts/autoload/data.gd`)
Constant dictionaries `WEAPONS`, `SKINS`, `HATS`, `OUTFITS`, `CHARMS`, `EMOTES`, `DRAWS`, `ATTACHMENTS`, `MAPS`. Item ids are snake_case strings.
Helpers: `Data.catalog(category) -> Dictionary` for category in `"weapons"|"skins"|"hats"|"outfits"|"tacticals"|"charms"|"emotes"|"draws"`; `Data.attachment_mult(id, level) -> float`.
Per-weapon stats: `STAT_EXTRA_SOURCES` (the non-weapon kill sources tracked, `grenade` and `bomber`).
`MODES` lists the game modes in match-setup order (`ffa`, `tdm`, `gungame`, `koth`, `infected`, `tactical`: `name`, `short` button label, `desc`, `teams` for two-team modes, optional `team_names` / `team_colors`); `TACTICAL_MAP` is the map Tactical plays (in `MAPS`, not in `MAP_ROTATION`).

### Profile (autoload, `scripts/autoload/profile.gd`)
- `signal changed`: emitted on any change to coins, inventory, equipment, or settings.
- Fields: `player_name`, `coins`, `xp`, `level`, `owned: Dictionary` (category -> Array[String]), `loadout: Dictionary` (`primary`, `secondary`, `tactical`), `equipped: Dictionary` (`skin`, `hat`, `outfit`, `charm`, `emote`, `draw`; slot -> category in `EQUIP_SLOTS`), `upgrades: Dictionary` (attachment id -> level), `settings: Dictionary`, `stats: Dictionary`, `weapon_stats: Dictionary` (id -> `WEAPON_STAT_KEYS` totals), `bindings: Dictionary` (action -> codes, overrides only). In team modes `World._s_match_end` passes the row's `won` (set by the mode's `server_finish`: every member of the winning team; in Infected the side that won, i.e. the survivors left at the end or every Infected) and placement 1 for a win, 2 for a draw, none for a loss.

### Models (`scripts/models/models.gd`, `class_name Models`, static)

- MatchMode hooks it overrides: `prepare_info` (empty slots), `spawn_point` (staging rooms), `server_balance`, `server_human_joined` / `left` (bot takeovers), `server_on_kill` (gun drop, wipe check), `server_finish`, `clock_running`, `attacks_blocked`, `respawn_delay` (-1), `spawn_protection` (false), `has_next_match` (false), `on_local_death` (spectating), HUD hooks; plus `server_check_end()`, `server_winner()`.
- Bots: `bot_order(p) -> {pos, hold, sprint, look}`; `squad_summary()` for inspection.
- RPCs: `_s_phase(phase, seconds_left)`, `_s_spots([[pos, wid], ...])`, `_s_spot(index, pos, wid)` (a new index appends a spot), `_s_give(eid, slot, wid)`, `_s_markers(list)`; `_c_interact()`, `_c_marker(kind, pos)`, `_c_marker_remove(id)`.
- UI: `TacticalOverlay` (`ui/tactical_overlay.gd`, canvas layer 11: planning banner, team label, world markers, swap prompt, pick-up toasts, spectator bar; `is_blocking()`, `open_map()`, `close_map()`), `TacticalMap` (`ui/tactical_map.gd`, the floor plan). `WeaponPickup` (`scripts/game/weapon_pickup.gd`) draws one spot.
