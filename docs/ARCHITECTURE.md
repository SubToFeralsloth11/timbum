# Timbum: architecture

Timbum is a blocky FPS built with Godot 4.7 (GDScript, Forward+, Jolt): free-for-all deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Gun Game, King of the Block, Infected, or 25 v 25 Tactical (`Net.config.mode`).
Matches mix bots and humans. Humans join a host over ENet (LAN discovery, room code, IP), Steam or WebRTC.

## Design decisions

These were settled up front, choosing the standard option each time:

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Mode | Free-for-all deathmatch. First to the kill limit (default 20) or the most kills when time runs out (default 8 min). After a match: every peer's camera cuts to the winner (`World._start_win_cam`), whose avatar plays their win emote (`peer_info.emote`); in a team result the winner is the local player when their side won and they are alive, otherwise the winning side's top row for `World.WIN_SHOW` (4 s), then the results screen (final table, match highlights for best streak / most headshots / fewest deaths, an itemised coins/XP breakdown, and the next map/mutator/time of day, which the server decides at match end with `Net.plan_next_match()` and sends in `_s_match_end`), then that match `World.RESULTS_TIME` (16 s, including the emote) after the match ended, or as soon as a majority of the session's humans vote to skip (`World.vote_skip()`); the server broadcasts the vote count (`_s_skip_votes`, also sent to a joiner arriving mid-results) and every peer's results screen shows "N of M ready (K needed)" with M and K taken from `Net.players`. The Tab scoreboard stays available over the results screen (`Hud.show_results` puts it on top). The results screen also has primary/secondary pickers; a change is saved to the profile and sent with `Net.update_my_info()`, so it applies from the first spawn of the next match. |
| Mode controllers | Every mode's rules live in one node: `World.rules`, a `MatchMode` (`scripts/game/match_mode.gd`) that `MatchMode.create()` picks from `Net.config.mode` on every peer and World adds as its child (named after the mode, so the mode's own RPCs reach the same node everywhere; World creates it before the Hud, whose scoreboard asks it for columns, and adds it to the tree after). World, Hud and BotBrain never test the mode; they call the controller's hooks: team for a newcomer, the info a player spawns with, spawn point, spawn protection, respawn delay (negative: none), whether the clock runs and attacks are blocked, bot balance, joins and leaves, what a kill does, which weapon the server accepts a hit from (`weapon_allowed`), the end of the match (`server_finish(rows)` orders the final rows and returns what the results need, e.g. `{winner}`), whether a next match follows on a timer, the HUD's top bars and lines, the free-for-all race (`race_value` / `race_limit` / `race_text`: kills, Gun Game level or King of the Block points), compass pips, the scoreboard's stat columns (`score_columns` / `row_cells`), grouping and tags, the results title, banner and extra highlights, and bot goals (`bot_roam_goal`, `bot_min_range`, `bot_hold_area`). The base class is plain Deathmatch; `TeamDeathmatch`, `GunGame`, `KingOfTheBlock`, `Infected` and `Tactical` subclass it. A new mode adds a `Data.MODES` entry, a subclass and a case in `MatchMode.create()`; `Net.config.kill_limit` is each mode's limit (`Net.sanitize_config` sets the mode's default). Team rules every team mode shares are in `Teams` (`scripts/game/teams.gd`): `Teams.is_team_mode()` (`Data.MODES[mode].teams`; team-only features ask this), `Player.info.team` (0 / 1, set by the server at spawn, -1 in free-for-all), names and colours (a mode may rename its sides, e.g. Infected's SURVIVORS / INFECTED), `Teams.pick` (a human joins the side with fewer humans, a bot the side with fewer players), armbands and nametags (teammates' tags through walls). `World.is_enemy` is everyone else except teammates; `World._server_damage` ignores hits between teammates in every team mode (your own grenade still hurts you). |
| Team Deathmatch | `"tdm"`, `TeamDeathmatch` (`scripts/game/team_deathmatch.gd`). Deathmatch maps, respawns and mutators as in Deathmatch. Each kill of an enemy adds 1 to the killer's team score (`TeamDeathmatch.score`, kept by the server so a leaver's kills still count, sent with `_s_score(alpha, bravo)`, also to joiners). The first team to `Net.config.kill_limit` wins (setup offers 25-100; `Net.sanitize_config` defaults it to `DEFAULT_KILL_LIMIT` 50 for this mode), otherwise the higher score at the time limit; equal is a draw (`winner` -1). Bots: `World.server_balance_bots` adds each bot to the smaller team and, when a human joins, removes one from the bigger team (`MatchMode.bot_drop_team`). Spawns rank the map's spawns by distance to the nearest living enemy (capped at 45 m), +10 on the team's half of the map (the longer axis; ALPHA the low end), +8 with a teammate within 20 m, and rule out spots an enemy can see or within 8 m of one; the pick varies among the best three within 6 points. Results rows carry `team`, `alive` and `won` (the winning team first); the title shows the score. |
| Gun Game | `"gungame"`, `GunGame` (`scripts/game/gun_game.gd`), free-for-all on the deathmatch maps with respawns. One ladder for everyone (`GunGame.steps`): the guns of `FULL_LADDER` (SMG, Quad-Bit, AR-Cube, carbine, Tri-Cube, LMG, minigun, shotgun, Drum Buster, DMR, crossbow, sniper, rocket, Bitstorm MP, sawn-off, Tri-Tap, revolver, Whisper, Block 9), then the knife (`GunGame.step_info` sets `info.melee` to the knife whatever the loadout holds, so the ladder's end and the knife-kill setback stay the knife's). `Net.config.kill_limit` is the ladder length: setup offers 6, 10, 15 or 20 steps (`LENGTHS`; default and maximum `max_length()` = 20); a shorter ladder keeps the first and last gun and spreads the rest evenly (`GunGame.ladder`). A kill of another player moves the killer up one step; a knife kill (also the quick melee) moves the victim down one; suicides and falls change nothing. The first knife kill on the last step ends the match; at the time limit the highest step wins (then kills, then fewest deaths). The step is `Player.info.ladder`; `step_info` builds the info for it (the step's gun in its own slot, the other slot empty, both empty on the knife step, `no_grenades`). The server sends `_s_step(eid, step, by)`; every peer applies it with `Player.apply_info`, and the promoted player (local or bot) draws the new gun with `Player.switch_to`, so the draw animation plays and the gun can't fire before `switch_time()`. Late joiners get the step inside each entity's info. Grenades are off (frags and tacticals: `info.no_grenades`, see `Player.carries_grenades`; the server's per-life counts are 0 and grenade crates skip them), so no kill skips the ladder; loadouts and loadout mutators (Rocket Party, Snipers Only) are ignored and never rolled. Hit validation (`GunGame.weapon_allowed`) takes the knife, the step's gun and, for `SWAP_GRACE` (0.6 s) after a promotion, the gun it replaced (shots and rockets already in flight). HUD: a ladder card (`GunGameCard`, `ui/gun_game_card.gd`, top left: LEVEL x / N, the gun now and next, a pip per step), the race on level, and announcements for promotions, setbacks and whoever reaches the knife. The scoreboard shows LEVEL / KILLS / DEATHS sorted by level; the results add "won it with the knife" and the most knife setbacks. Bots use whatever gun their step gives them and, on the knife step, hunt the nearest player (`bot_roam_goal`). Rewards: the first row (the knife winner, or the highest level at time-up) is `won`. |
| King of the Block | `"koth"`, `KingOfTheBlock` (`scripts/game/king_of_the_block.gd`), free-for-all on the deathmatch maps with respawns. Zone spots (`KingOfTheBlock.zone_spots`) are the map's health/ammo/grenade pickup spots, at least 10 m apart (5-9 per map), all with a navmesh path from every spawn (checked on all 8 maps). The zone is a 4 m-radius cylinder from 1 m below to 3.5 m above its floor. The server alone decides: one living player alone in it holds it and scores a point per full second held; two or more contest it and nobody scores; the hold progress restarts whenever the holder changes. Every `MOVE_INTERVAL` (60 s) of match clock the zone moves to a random other spot; `WARNING` (10 s) before, every peer announces it and shows the next spot faintly. `Net.config.kill_limit` is the points to win (setup offers 60-200, default `DEFAULT_SCORE_LIMIT` 120); otherwise the most points at the time limit win (then kills). RPCs: `_s_zone(index, next, move_at)` (`move_at` is the `World.time_left` of the next move, so every peer counts down from the synced clock), `_s_hold(holder, contested, progress)`, `_s_point(eid, total, progress)`, and `_s_state(...)` with all of it for joiners. The zone is drawn on every peer as an additive glowing ring, floor disc and tall beam, coloured free (gold) / yours (green) / someone else's (red) / contested (pulsing magenta). `ZoneOverlay` (`ui/zone_overlay.gd`) draws the zone marker with its distance (on the screen edge when off screen, plus the next spot during the warning) and a card: holder, CONTESTED, the capture progress bar, your points and the move countdown; the compass gets a pip (`compass_pips`). Scoreboard: POINTS / KILLS / DEATHS by points; the results add the longest hold. Bots head for their own point inside the zone (`bot_roam_goal`), and while it exists (`bot_hold_area`) they fight on their way in, never chase out of it, and only close in on targets inside it. Rewards: the first row (most points) is `won`. |
| Infected | `"infected"`, `Infected` (`scripts/game/infected.gd`). Everyone starts a Survivor (team 0). `START_DELAY` (10 s) into the match the server turns a random living player Infected (`_s_role(eid, role, first)`); a Survivor who dies for any reason turns Infected at the kill (after the kill feed shows them as a Survivor) and respawns as one. An Infected's info (`Infected.infect_info`) has team 1, empty primary/secondary (their own melee weapon stays in `info.melee`, so an Infected swings the knife, bat, axe, hammer or katana they equipped; its `move_mult` applies on top of the Infected speed), `knife_only` (no grenades or tactical: the server's per-life counts are 0 and grenade crates skip them), `max_hp` 150 and `speed` 1.15; `Player.apply_info` rebuilds slots and speed on every peer, and the avatar gets a green `Avatar.set_tint`. The server ends the match when no Survivor is left (Infected win) or at the time limit (Survivors win); if every Infected leaves, a new one is picked. Joiners (and new bots) after the start are Infected; a human joining pushes out an Infected bot first, never the last of a side. Loadout mutators (Rocket Party, Snipers Only) are never rolled or accepted. Rows carry `team`, `won` (the winning side) and `infections`; the results list the survivors and the top infector instead of "fewest deaths". Infected bots always head for the nearest Survivor (`bot_roam_goal`); Survivor bots keep 9 m away from an Infected (`bot_min_range`). |
| Tactical mode | `Net.config.mode == "tactical"` (`Data.MODES`). Always `Data.TACTICAL_MAP` (Block HQ), 50 players (`Tactical.PLAYERS`) and no mutators; `Net.sanitize_config` forces these. Its rules are the `Tactical` mode controller (`scripts/game/tactical.gd`, a `MatchMode`, World's child "Tactical"; `World.tactical` points to it for its own UI): teams, spawns, bot balance, kills and the end of the match. **Teams:** `Player.info.team` (0 ALPHA west, 1 BRAVO east), set by the server at spawn (`Teams`). A human joins the side with fewer humans; bots fill each side to 25 (`TEAM_SIZE`). Bots are only added during planning: a human arriving later takes over a living bot of their side (position, health, weapons) or spectates if none is left; a human who leaves alive is replaced by a bot the same way. **Phases:** PLANNING (30 s, `PLANNING_TIME`): translucent barriers (a `StaticBody3D` on layer 1 over `building_map.gd` `BARRIERS`) close each staging room, `World.attacks_blocked()` stops firing, knifing and throwing (client and server) and the match clock does not run; then FIGHT (`_s_phase`), then OVER. **One life:** no respawns and no spawn protection; `World._server_damage` ignores hits between teammates (your own grenade still hurts you). The match ends when a side has nobody alive (`Tactical.server_check_end`, after every kill and leave) or at the time limit (more alive wins; equal is a draw). `_s_match_end` rows then also carry `team`, `alive` and `won` (the winning side first, survivors before the fallen), `next` is `{winner}` (0, 1, -1 for a draw) and `wait` is `WIN_SHOW + 2`: every camera watches the winning side's top survivor perform their win emote under a "<TEAM> WINS!" banner (skipped on a draw), then the results screen shows. Nothing advances on a timer: its NEW GAME button (host or offline only; clients wait or leave) calls `Net.next_match()`. A team win counts as victory (`Profile.record_match` placement 1), a draw pays like second place. **Found weapons:** slots start empty (`""`). Weapon spots are the map's pickups of kind `"weapon"` plus the gun each dead player drops; each holds one weapon id the server rolls (`WEAPON_WEIGHTS`: launchers and snipers rare). Standing on a spot with that slot empty takes it (bots also swap up by `BOT_RATING`); the `interact` key swaps it for the gun in that slot (`_c_interact`). `_s_give(eid, slot, wid)` updates `Player.slots` and `info` on every peer, so the server's hit check (weapon in the shooter's `Player.slots`) accepts picked-up guns and rejects any other; `_s_spot` updates the spot. A picked-up gun comes with a full magazine (`Player.mag_size`, so Extended Mag applies) and the standard reserve. **Planning tools:** the floor plan (`TacticalMap`, `tactical_map` key, opened automatically at the start) shows each storey with living teammates and weapon spots; clicks place ATTACK and HOLD markers (`_c_marker`, at most 3 per player, the oldest replaced). The server sends each peer only its own team's markers (`_s_markers`); the HUD draws them in the world (`TacticalOverlay`) and on the compass (`Hud.Compass.pips`). **Bot squads:** when planning ends the server groups each side's bots into squads of 5, each on a different shuffled route from `building_map.gd` `ROUTES` (continued through the mirrored east half into the enemy staging room). A squad moves on from a waypoint once 60% of its armed members are within 6 m and it has held there 3-6 s (longer after contact; never more than 25 s on one waypoint), crouching while it holds and spread across the objective. ATTACK markers pull the two nearest free squads (sprinting), HOLD markers one (digging in there). With 4 or fewer enemies left, or 3 minutes in, squads hunt the nearest enemy. For 35 s after the barriers drop an unarmed bot first heads for the nearest unclaimed weapon spot (`_claim_spot`). `BotBrain` rereads its order every 0.3 s (`Tactical.bot_order`), targets only enemies (`World.is_enemy`) and still chases a fresh sighting within 18 m. **Pacing:** no grapple hook and no sliding (Player and BotBrain skip both when `world.tactical` is set). **Spectating:** 3 s after death the local view moves to `Tactical`'s SpectateCam, over the shoulder of a living teammate (anyone alive once the team is gone); fire/aim cycle. Teams show as coloured armbands on both arms, team-coloured nametags (teammates' through walls) and kill feed names, and living-player bars per team in the HUD's top panel. **Performance:** on this Apple Silicon Mac (8 GB), a 50-player offline match at the default (Medium) preset, windowed 1280x720 with vsync off, averaged 108-117 FPS (display-capped near 120) with 1% lows of 36-49 FPS in the heaviest firefights; headless (simulation only) it runs at about 145 FPS. Weapon spots stop drawing past 32 m and bots read squad orders at 3 Hz; no further think-rate LOD was needed. |
| Players | 2-12 per match. Bots fill up to the target player count (default 8). When a human joins, one bot leaves (a dead bot if possible, otherwise one nobody is fighting, then the lowest scorer); when a human leaves, a bot comes back. Respawns rank spawns by distance to the nearest living player, with any spawn a living player can see ranked below every hidden one; the pick varies among the best three, but only between spawns as hidden as the best one and at least 5 m from any living player. |
| Networking | ENet, default port **7777**. The host is the server and also plays. Clients run their own movement and hit detection (trusted, no server-side line-of-sight check). The server owns health, kills, score, pickups, bots, projectiles, and the match clock. It sanity-checks every hit request: shooter and target alive, weapon allowed by the mode (`MatchMode.weapon_allowed`: a weapon in the shooter's current slots, `Player.slots`: the loadout with its melee weapon, in Tactical whatever they picked up, in Gun Game the step's gun) and not a projectile weapon (rockets only deal damage through the server's own projectile), a fire-rate budget per shooter shared by all guns (each shot costs its weapon's refire interval, with slack for 3 bunched shots, or a whole burst for a burst weapon longer than that: the Quad-Bit's 4; melee weapons share their own budget), total damage per target capped at one shot's maximum, and melee range (the weapon's `range` plus `Data.MELEE_SERVER_SLACK` 2.1 m between the two players' feet). Spawning grants 2 s of protection from other players' damage (`World.SPAWN_PROTECTION`); firing a shot, rocket or grenade ends your own protection early. The server announces it with `_s_protection(eid, seconds)`, which every peer shows as a pulsing cyan shell on the avatar (`Avatar.set_protected`) and the protected player sees as a "SPAWN PROTECTED" countdown above the crosshair. Offline play uses `OfflineMultiplayerPeer`, so the same code runs everywhere. `Net.PROTOCOL_VERSION` must match between host and client; the host kicks mismatched clients. |
| Connections | `Net.host(port, cfg, link)` / `Net.join(address, port)` / `Net.join_code(code)` / `Net.join_steam_lobby(id)`, built on `addons/netlink`. `Link.DIRECT` is one ENet server reachable four ways: `NetLinkLan` answers UDP discovery queries on port 47777 (Join lists the host), `NetLinkNoray` registers the ENet socket with the public noray server (`tomfol.io:8890`) for a room code (the noray open id; NAT punch-through first, relay on failure, and `Net` retries a failed direct link through the relay), and addresses (LAN, Tailscale, ZeroTier, hostnames) still work. `Link.STEAM` uses a Steam lobby (Spacewar app 480) and GodotSteam's `SteamMultiplayerPeer` (`addons/godotsteam`); the lobby id is the code and overlay invites call `Net.join_steam_lobby`. `Link.WEBRTC` uses `WebRTCMultiplayerPeer` (`addons/webrtc`, webrtc-native) with PeerJS signalling and Google STUN; codes are `W-` plus 6 letters. Every MultiplayerPeer is server-relayed, so gameplay code never knows which link is in use. The host's pre-match lobby and pause menu show how to join (`InviteBox`, `ui/invite_box.gd`: `Net.room_code`, `Net.invite_changed`). |
| Pre-match lobby | `Net.host` opens a lobby (`Net.open_lobby`, scene `scenes/lobby.tscn`, `LobbyScreen` in `ui/lobby_screen.gd`) instead of loading the first map; `play_offline` never does. While `Net.in_lobby`, `_rpc_register` sends a new peer `_rpc_enter_lobby(config, lobby)` instead of `_rpc_load_match`; once the first match has loaded, joiners go straight into it as before. The state is `Net.lobby` `{options, ready, votes, countdown}`, server-authoritative: clients send `_rpc_lobby_ready(on)`, `_rpc_lobby_vote(index)` and `_rpc_lobby_chat(text)`; after every change (and every join or leave) `_server_lobby_update` drops peers that left, starts the match when every human is ready, runs a `LOBBY_COUNTDOWN` (10 s) countdown while a majority (`Net.majority`, as the results skip vote) but not everyone is ready (cancelled when that drops), and sends everyone `_rpc_lobby_state`; `countdown` is the seconds left when sent, each screen counts it down itself and the server's `_process` starts the match on time. The host's START NOW (`Net.lobby_start`) starts at once. The map vote offers `MAP_VOTE_CHOICES` (3) maps of the mode's pool plus `RANDOM_MAP`, always including the host's own map pick; the most votes wins (`Net.vote_winner`), RANDOM picks any pool map, no votes or a tie play the host's pick (or the rotation's next map), and a voted map leaves the current rotation round. Tactical has no vote (one map). The lobby chat is a separate small relay (`_rpc_lobby_said`, sanitized like the match chat; join and leave lines are system messages) because the match chat lives on World and the Hud, which don't exist yet. The screen lists players (ready, prestige badge, level, HOST), the vote cards with live counts, the rules (`MatchSetupScreen.describe_config`) and, for the host, the `InviteBox`. Protocol 14. |
| Next-map vote | At match end `World._server_end_match` adds `map_options` to the `next` dictionary of `_s_match_end` (`Net.next_map_options()`: the planned next map first, then two other pool maps, never the map just played; none in Tactical). Peers vote with `World.vote_map(index)` (`_c_vote_map`); the server keeps `_map_votes` (peer -> index) and broadcasts `_s_map_votes(counts)` on every vote, join and leave, like the skip vote's `_s_skip_votes`, so every player (voter or not) sees the counts on the three buttons. When the results end (timer or skip vote), `_server_advance` applies the winner with `Net.vote_next_map(map)` if it isn't the planned map (`Net.vote_winner`: most votes; a tie or no votes keeps the planned map); in a rotation the picked map leaves the rotation and the planned one goes to its end. The mutator and time of day announced stay. Works offline too (one voter). |
| Art | All art is procedural; the only external assets are the music and sound-effect files (see Audio). Voxel maps use 1 m blocks, a 16x16 pixel-art texture atlas, vertex ambient occlusion, and ramps for elevation changes. The block shader (`BlockAtlas._SHADER_CODE`) bends normals by each texel's brightness (pixel-art relief, fading out past 35 m) and reads per-tile surface properties from the `layer_props` table (`BlockAtlas._SURFACES`: smoothness, metallic, foliage sway, water ripple); its `detail` uniform (the `block_detail` setting, `BlockAtlas.set_detail`) switches the relief, sway and ripples off. `PostProcess` (a canvas layer under the HUD) sharpens, grades per time of day and vignettes; the `post_fx` setting hides it. Video settings apply live through `Profile._apply_graphics`: `anti_aliasing` (off, FXAA, MSAA 2x/4x, TAA) and `render_scale` on the root viewport (`_apply_render_scale`, re-run on every window resize: `scaling_3d_scale` = `render_scale` × min(1, √(`RENDER_PIXEL_BUDGET` 1600×900 / window pixels)), upscaled with FSR 1 below native), and `shadows`, `ambient_occlusion`, `bloom` and `reflections` on every map sun and `WorldEnvironment` in the `MapBuilder.GRAPHICS_GROUP` group (`MapBuilder.apply_graphics`). The `quality` preset sets view distance, shadow detail and SSIL when a map is built, and picking it in Settings switches those toggles to `Profile.QUALITY_PRESETS`. Characters, guns, and hats are built from boxes. |
| Audio | Sound effects are WAV files in `audio/sfx/` (48 kHz 16-bit stereo, made in SoundCraft by `sfx_pack/soundcraft_sfx.py`; imported with trailing silence trimmed). `Sfx` loads every take when it starts (about 9 MB, loaded once, so no sound ever waits on a render); headless runs load nothing. Only the results fanfare (a chiptune) is still synthesised, into an `AudioStreamWAV` on a WorkerThreadPool task (`AudioJob`) at startup. The menu, shop and match music are MP3 songs in `audio/music/` (`Sfx.MUSIC_FILES`: per track a first song and a playlist). Songs play once; the music player's `finished` signal crossfades to the next song of the track's shuffled playlist (never the one that just ended), so the music runs song to song for as long as the track is on. |
| Variety | Map rotation is shuffled each cycle. Each match the server rolls a time of day (day/sunset/night) and, when mutators are on, a mutator half the time (`Data.MUTATORS`: low gravity, turbo, big heads, vampire, rocket party, snipers only, glass cannon). Both travel to clients inside `Net.config`. Loadout mutators are applied by the server at spawn (`World._apply_loadout_mutator`): the mutator's gun (`World.LOADOUT_MUTATORS`: rocket for Rocket Party, sniper for Snipers Only) becomes the primary and the player's own primary moves into the secondary slot (a player whose primary already is that gun keeps the sidearm); Rocket Party also doubles the rocket's reserve and crate refill (`Player._ammo_scale`). Bots holding a sniper or rocket switch to their secondary (unless it is a long gun too) inside `BotBrain.LONG_GUN_CLOSE` (12 m / 9 m) and back beyond it plus `LONG_GUN_SLACK`. |
| Movement | Slide: crouch pressed while sprinting on the ground above `Player.SLIDE_MIN_ENTRY` starts a slide at `SLIDE_SPEED` (or current speed + `SLIDE_BOOST`) that bleeds `SLIDE_FRICTION`, gains speed downhill, steers a little and ends on release, a wall, low speed or `SLIDE_MAX_TIME`; jumping keeps its speed. Peek: `lean` (-1..1) moves the eye `LEAN_DIST` sideways and rolls the camera, clamped by a sphere cast so the eye never enters a wall; head and body hitboxes follow, and remotes get the lean quantised into state-flag bits 16-19 (`FLAG_SLIDE` carries the slide). Prone: the "prone" action toggles `Player.prone` (on the ground, not grappling; a slide can dive into it). The collider shrinks to `PRONE_HEIGHT`, the eye drops to `PRONE_EYE`, speed is `PRONE_SPEED`, spread x0.6 and recoil x0.65; the body hitbox lies flat (`PRONE_BODY`) reaching back along the legs. Crouch, jump or prone again gets up into a crouch if there is headroom (the jump press does not also jump). No sprint, peek, slide or grapple while prone. Remotes get it as `FLAG_PRONE`; the avatar tips face down (`Avatar.PRONE_LIFT`/`PRONE_BACK`) with head and aim tilted back up. Grapple: a world ray up to `GRAPPLE_RANGE` (never the invisible `Bounds`) anchors a rope that pulls the player in with damped swing until close, timed out, blocked or released; the anchor reaches other peers through `World.report_grapple` -> `_c_grapple` -> `_s_grapple`. Movement stays client-trusted like the rest. |
| Pause | In an offline (vs bots) match the pause menu freezes the match: `World.set_frozen` pauses the scene tree (and physics) and stops `World.now()`, the match clock every timer reads (respawns, pickups, smoke, flashes, spawn protection), so nothing jumps ahead on resume. The pause menu runs with `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`; so does `Sfx`, but its positional (3D) voices are `PROCESS_MODE_PAUSABLE`, so world sounds pause with the tree and resume with it (flat UI voices and music keep playing). Online matches keep running. The Hud opens the pause menu on `NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_FOCUS_OUT` and on the window's `focus_exited` (window focus-out notifications don't reach child nodes; minimising also fires it), unless it is already open, the results screen, win cam or photo mode is up (`Hud._on_focus_lost`). The menu sits on its own canvas layer `Hud.PAUSE_LAYER` (50), above the Hud (10), every overlay (11: streaks, Tactical, range, zones, pings, spectator, tutorial and Gun Game cards), the kill cam (9) and photo mode (20). `Profile` mutes the Master bus while the root window is unfocused when the `mute_unfocused` setting is on (`window_focused`, `_apply_master`), in menus too. |
| Spectators | A spectator is a peer whose `Net.players[pid].spectator` is true (`Net.sanitize_info` keeps only a real bool; `_apply_info` keeps the server's value, so only `Net.set_spectating` changes it). The server gives a spectator **no entity**: nothing to render, hit, collide with or pick anything up, and `_server_shot` / `_c_state` find no shooter. It takes no player slot: `World.server_balance_bots` fills `bots_fill - Net.playing_count()`, `Net._rpc_register` admits players up to `Data.MAX_PLAYERS` and spectators up to `Net.MAX_SPECTATORS` (4) on top. ## Rendering the viewmodel

Everything uses visual layer 1. The first-person arms and gun (`scripts/game/viewmodel.gd`) swap their materials for a shader that squashes depth toward the near plane, so the gun draws over walls instead of clipping into them while still being lit by the scene.

The local player's own avatar uses `cast_shadow = SHADOW_CASTING_SETTING_SHADOWS_ONLY`.

## Contracts between modules

### Data (autoload, `scripts/autoload/data.gd`)
Constant dictionaries `WEAPONS`, `SKINS`, `HATS`, `OUTFITS`, `CHARMS`, `EMOTES`, `DRAWS`, `SPRAYS`, `ATTACHMENTS`, `MAPS`. Item ids are snake_case strings.
Helpers: `Data.is_melee(wid) -> bool` (slot "melee"); `Data.catalog(category) -> Dictionary` for category in `"weapons"|"skins"|"hats"|"outfits"|"tacticals"|"charms"|"emotes"|"draws"|"sprays"`; `Data.attachment_mult(id, level) -> float`.
Progression constants: `MASTERY_TIERS` (`{name, kills, coins, color}`), `STAT_EXTRA_SOURCES`, `LEVEL_CAP`, `PRESTIGE_MAX`, `PRESTIGE_COINS`; static helpers `mastery_tier_for(kills)`, `mastery_skin(wid)`, `is_gun_skin(skin_id)`, `gun_skin_for(info, wid)`, `prestige_badge(rank)`, `badged_name(name, rank)`.
Challenges: `CHALLENGE_TEMPLATES` (id -> `{text: [one, many] with {n}/{p}, key (MatchFacts.tally counter, "%s" = param), param ("gun"|"gun_hs"|"mode"|"map"|""), group, daily: [min, max], weekly: [min, max], per_match}`), `CHALLENGE_SLOTS`, `CHALLENGE_REWARDS`, `LONG_KILL_DISTANCE`.
Achievements and medals: `MEDALS` (id -> `{name, desc, mode}`), `ACHIEVEMENTS` (id -> `{name, desc, key, target}`), `ACHIEVE_ALL`.
`MODES` lists the game modes in match-setup order (`ffa`, `tdm`, `gungame`, `koth`, `infected`, `tactical`: `name`, `short` button label, `desc`, `teams` for two-team modes, optional `team_names` / `team_colors` (Palette role names, e.g. Infected's `survivors` / `infected`)); `TACTICAL_MAP` is the map Tactical plays (in `MAPS`, not in `MAP_ROTATION`).

### Profile (autoload, `scripts/autoload/profile.gd`)
- `signal changed`: emitted on any change to coins, inventory, equipment, or settings.
- Fields: `player_name`, `coins`, `xp`, `level`, `prestige` (0-10), `owned: Dictionary` (category -> Array[String]), `loadout: Dictionary` (`primary`, `secondary`, `tactical`), `equipped: Dictionary` (`skin`, `hat`, `outfit`, `charm`, `emote`, `draw`, `spray`; slot -> category in `EQUIP_SLOTS`), `upgrades: Dictionary` (attachment id -> level), `attachments_off: Array` (attachment ids switched off in the locker; `is_attachment_on(id)`, `set_attachment_on(id, on)`, `active_mods()`; old saves: none, so all on), `settings: Dictionary`, `stats: Dictionary`, `weapon_stats: Dictionary` (id -> `WEAPON_STAT_KEYS` totals), `mastery_paid: Dictionary` (weapon id -> tiers paid), `camos: Array` (weapon ids wearing their mastery camo), `bindings: Dictionary` (action -> codes, overrides only). Saves without the newer fields load with them empty / 0.
- `challenges: Dictionary`: `"daily"` / `"weekly"` -> `{period, list}`, entries `{id, param, n, target, progress, claimed}` (`n` is the number in the text: the target, or a per-match threshold with target 1). Loading repairs each entry (`Challenges.sanitize`) and drops unknown ones; saves without it get today's sets at the first refresh. Saved and exported with the rest.
- Challenges: `refresh_challenges(day := -1, pay := true) -> {coins, xp}` (rolls over ended periods; called by the main menu, the Challenges screen, `World._ready` and `record_match`), `challenge_list(kind)`, `claimable_challenges()`, `claim_challenge(kind, index) -> {coins, xp, levels}` (`{}` unless complete and unclaimed: pays once), `preview_feats(result) -> [[key, title, text], ...]` (what a result would complete; changes nothing), `owned_guns()`.
- Achievements and medals: `feats: Dictionary` (lifetime counters, keys checked by `_is_feat_key`), `achievements: Dictionary` (id -> unlock Unix time); `achievement_value(key, tally := {})`, `achievement_target(id)`, `achievement_progress(id) -> [value, target]`, `medal_count(id)`. Both are saved and exported; loading keeps known keys and ids only.
- Per-weapon stats and mastery: static `stat_sources()`, `weapon_stat(wid, key)`, static `match_summary(per_weapon) -> {shots, hits, accuracy, most_used}`, `mastery_tier(wid)` (0-3), `mastery_text(wid)`, `has_camo(wid)`, `is_camo_on(wid)`, `set_camo(wid, on) -> bool`, `gun_skin(wid)`. Prestige: `can_prestige()`, `prestige_bonus()`, `do_prestige() -> int` (coins paid, -1 when refused).
- `is_owned(category, id) -> bool`, `buy(category, id) -> bool`, `equip(category, id) -> bool`. Equipping a weapon sets the loadout slot from `Data.WEAPONS[id].slot`.
- Attachments: `upgrade_level(id)`, `upgrade_price(id)` (-1 when maxed), `is_upgrade_maxed(id)`, `buy_upgrade(id) -> bool`.
- Backup: `export_to(path) -> Error` writes the whole profile as JSON; `import_from(path) -> String` replaces it ("" on success, else a message; nothing changes on failure).
- Match setup presets: `match_presets: Array` of `{name, settings}` (settings: `MatchSetupScreen` choices), `MAX_MATCH_PRESETS` (8), `match_preset_index(name) -> int`, `save_match_preset(name, choice) -> int` (index; replaces a preset of the same name; -1 for an empty name or when full), `rename_match_preset(index, name) -> bool` (false for a bad index, an empty name or one another preset has), `delete_match_preset(index) -> bool`. Each saves the profile. Loading drops entries without a name, repeated names and any past the limit, and sanitizes the settings.
- Loadout presets (see "Loadout presets"): `loadout_presets: Array` of `{name, primary, secondary, tactical}`, `active_preset`, `LOADOUT_PRESET_COUNT` (3), `DEFAULT_LOADOUT`, `LOADOUT_KEYS`; `select_preset(index) -> bool` (copies it into `loadout`), `rename_preset(index, name) -> bool` (false for a bad index or an empty name), `preset_summary(index)` ("Bit-SMG · Block 9 · Flash Cube"), static `default_preset_name(index)`, `fresh_presets(base)`. `equip()` of a weapon or tactical also edits the active preset. Both save the profile and emit `changed`.
- `can_claim_daily() -> bool`, `claim_daily() -> int`, `xp_for_level(level) -> int`.
- `record_match(result: Dictionary) -> Dictionary`. Takes `{kills, deaths, headshots, placement, players, won, best_streak, weapons}` (`weapons`: the match's per-weapon numbers) and returns `{coins, xp, levels_gained, breakdown, shots, hits, accuracy, most_used, mastery}`; `breakdown` is `[[label, coins, xp], ...]`, one row per reward source (mastery tiers included), which the results screen lists; `accuracy` is -1 without shots, `mastery` the `[wid, tier]` pairs reached. A win (victory XP and the `wins` stat) only counts when `players > 1`. In team modes `World._s_match_end` passes the row's `won` (set by the mode's `server_finish`: every member of the winning team; in Infected the side that won, i.e. the survivors left at the end or every Infected) and placement 1 for a win, 2 for a draw, none for a loss.
- `record_match` also takes `mode`, `map`, `moves` and `best_multi` (`MatchFacts.result`), advances the challenges with `MatchFacts.tally(result)` and returns `feats`: the `[key, title, text]` entries it completed (World toasts those not already toasted live).
- `get_setting(key)`, `set_setting(key, value)` (applies the setting and saves), `apply_settings()`, `save()`. Controller settings: `pad_sens`, `pad_invert_y`, `pad_deadzone` (0-0.5), `pad_curve` (`PAD_CURVES` index), `aim_assist` (0-2). Crosshair settings: the `crosshair_*` keys (see "Crosshair"), clamped on load; saves without them get the defaults. `killcam` (bool, default on): the kill cam replay.
- Input: `get_binding(action)`, `set_binding(action, index, code)` (key/mouse codes), `get_pad_binding(action)`, `set_pad_binding(action, code)` (pad codes; "" unbinds), `reset_bindings()` (both), `key_text(action)`, `pad_stick(left, right, up, down) -> Vector2`, `using_pad`, `signal device_changed`, static `code_text(code)`, `binding_text(event)`, `event_from_code(code)`, `code_from_event(event)`, `is_bindable(code)`, `is_pad_code(code)`, `pad_may_share(a, b)`. `pad_bindings` is saved and exported with `bindings`. Tactical adds the actions `interact` (B / X, swap weapon) and `tactical_map` (M / D-pad up, floor plan). `skip_killcam` (K / A, shared with `jump` in `PAD_SHARED`) skips the kill cam. `spectate_view` (N / X, shared with `reload` and `interact`: a spectator has no gun) switches a spectator between the free camera and following; `photo_mode` (P / Share) opens photo mode offline; `inspect` (I, no pad default) plays the weapon inspect; `spray` (Y, no pad default) paints the equipped spray (`Sprays`).
- `peer_info() -> Dictionary`: what this player sends to the server, `{name, outfit, hat, skin, charm, emote, draw, spray, mods, primary, secondary, tactical, level, prestige, camos}`.

### Sfx (autoload, `scripts/autoload/sfx.gd`)
- `Sfx.play(name: StringName, pos: Variant = null, volume_db := 0.0, pitch := 1.0)`. Pass a `Vector3` for positional 3D sound or `null` for a flat UI sound. Unknown names are ignored with a warning (once per name).
- Sound files: `audio/sfx/<name>.wav`, or `<name>_<take>.wav` (takes 0 to N-1) for the names in `Sfx.VARIANTS` (`footstep` 4, `bullet_impact` 3, `knife_hit` 2, `grenade_bounce` 2); a random take plays each time. Per name, `Sfx.GAIN_DB` sets the playback gain (loudness-matched, see `sfx_pack/README.md`), `Sfx.UNIT_SIZE` the positional reference distance (`DEFAULT_UNIT_SIZE` 7 m) and `Sfx.JITTER` a ±4 % random pitch. A new sound = a WAV plus its name in `Sfx.NAMES` and a `GAIN_DB` entry.
- `Sfx.play_music(track: StringName)` with `&"menu"`, `&"shop"` (the shop screen plays it while open, then goes back to `&"menu"`), `&"match"`, or `&"results"`. `Sfx.stop_music()`.
- Buses `Master`, `Music`, `SFX` (`default_bus_layout.tres`).
- Required sound names: `shot_pistol shot_revolver shot_smg shot_rifle shot_shotgun shot_sniper shot_lmg shot_rocket shot_suppressed shot_dmr shot_crossbow shot_drum_shotgun shot_carbine shot_burst_smg shot_minigun swing_blunt swing_heavy swing_blade explosion smoke_pop flashbang slide grapple_fire reload dry_fire knife_swing knife_hit hit_marker headshot kill_confirm hurt death footstep jump land pickup_health pickup_ammo grenade_throw grenade_bounce weapon_switch ui_click ui_hover ui_buy ui_error level_up coins countdown match_start match_end bullet_impact whizz spawn streak_reward radar_sweep shield_hit shield_break bomber_warning ping ping_enemy spray`. A weapon's optional `pitch` field shifts its shot sound.

### Net (autoload, `scripts/autoload/net.gd`)
- `const DEFAULT_PORT := 7777`
- `play_offline(config: Dictionary)`, `host(port: int, config: Dictionary) -> Error`, `join(ip: String, port: int) -> Error`, `leave()` (closes the session and returns to the main menu).
- `config` keys: `mode` (a `Data.MODES` id: `"ffa"`, `"tdm"`, `"gungame"`, `"koth"`, `"infected"` or `"tactical"`), `map` (a `Data.MAP_ROTATION` id or `"rotation"`), `bots_fill` (target total players, int), `difficulty` (0 easy, 1 normal, 2 hard), `kill_limit` (the mode's limit: kills; Team Deathmatch: the team score, default 50; Gun Game: the ladder length, 2-15, default 15; King of the Block: the points to win, default 120), `time_limit` (seconds), `mutators` (bool), `streaks` (bool, killstreak rewards; missing: `Streaks.default_for(mode)`). The server adds `mutator` and `time_of_day` per match before sending the config to clients. Tactical forces `map` = `Data.TACTICAL_MAP`, `bots_fill` = 50 and no mutators; Infected and Gun Game never get a loadout mutator (`World.LOADOUT_MUTATORS`).
- `config.session`: a `Data.SESSIONS` id, `"match"` by default; `"range"` is the offline Firing Range (`play_range()`; see "Firing Range"), which `sanitize_config` pins to `Data.RANGE_MAP`, Deathmatch, daylight, no mutators or killstreaks. Hosting always plays `"match"`.
- `signal status(text: String)` reports connection progress or errors to the menu.
- `players: Dictionary` maps peer id to `peer_info`, kept on every peer. `update_my_info()` resends this player's `Profile.peer_info()` (the server keeps the name it registered and the spectator flag).
- Server only: `plan_next_match() -> {map, mutator, time_of_day}` decides the next match once per match; `next_match()` loads exactly that. `next_map_options() -> Array` (the results screen's map vote, planned map first) and `vote_next_map(map_id)` (the vote's pick replaces the planned map).
- Lobby (see "Pre-match lobby"): `in_lobby`, `lobby` (`{options, ready, votes, countdown}`), `signal lobby_changed`, `signal lobby_chat(sender, text)` ("" sender: system line); `lobby_set_ready(on)`, `lobby_vote(index)`, `lobby_say(text)` (any peer); server only `open_lobby()`, `lobby_start()`. Helpers: static `vote_winner(counts) -> int` (-1 on no votes or a tie), `vote_counts(votes, options) -> Array` (peers still connected), `lobby_ready_count()`, static `majority(humans)` (`humans / 2 + 1`; `World.skip_votes_needed` uses it).
- Spectators (see "Spectators"): `peer_info.spectator` (bool), `join_as_spectator` (sent with the registration), `MAX_SPECTATORS` (4), `signal spectate_requested(pid, on)` (server, in a match; World switches the player); `is_spectator(pid)`, `playing_count()` (peers taking a player slot), `spectator_ids()` (sorted), `set_spectating(on)` (any peer; lobby or match), server `server_set_spectating(pid, on) -> String` ("" or why it was refused). `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 20 (16: `World._s_loadout`, a loadout preset applied at the respawn; 17: `Player.FLAG_INSPECT` in the state flags; 18: `peer_info.spray` and the `Sprays` RPCs; 19: jump pads; 20: `peer_info.melee`, a melee argument on `World._s_loadout`, and the new weapon ids). `sanitize_info` replaces a `melee` that isn't a melee weapon with the knife (ownership isn't checked server-side, as for every other cosmetic and loadout field). `update_my_info()` may also run mid-match (a preset picked while dead); the server applies the loadout at the next spawn.

### MapBuilder (`scripts/world/map_builder.gd`, `class_name MapBuilder`)
- `static func build(map_id: String, root: Node3D, quality: int) -> MapInfo` adds the chunk meshes, trimesh collision (layer 1), `WorldEnvironment`, `DirectionalLight3D`, and a `NavigationRegion3D` with a baked mesh to `root`.
- `MapInfo` (`class_name MapInfo`, a `RefCounted`) has `id`, `display_name`, `spawns: Array[Vector3]` (feet positions), `pickups: Array[Dictionary]` (`{kind: "health"|"ammo"|"grenade"|"weapon", pos: Vector3}`; `"weapon"` spots are Tactical's and World skips them), `kill_y: float`, `bounds: AABB`, and `nav_map: RID`.
- `MapInfo.marks`: named spots a map's own controller reads (the range's `line`, `console`, `ledge`, `dummies`, `signs`): a `Vector3`, an `AABB` or an Array of Dictionaries with `pos`, all moved to world space by `build()`.
- `static func generate(map_id: String, info: MapInfo) -> VoxelGrid` runs the generator only: fills `info` (world-space spawns, pickups, marks, bounds) and returns the grid (cell (0,0,0) at `info.bounds.position`). `build()` uses it.
- `static func build_scenery(grid, info, root, quality, atmosphere: Dictionary, keep_chunk: Callable)` (menu backdrop): chunk meshes for the chunks `keep_chunk(cx, cz)` accepts plus sky, sun and fog, with `atmosphere` keys overriding the map's `ATMOSPHERE`; no collision, bounds, navigation or jump pads. `VoxelMesher.build` takes a null `nav_source` (meshes only) and the optional `keep_chunk` for this.
- `static func preview(map_id: String) -> ImageTexture` returns a cached top-down image of the map for menus. `root` must be in the scene tree when calling `build()`.

### Models (`scripts/models/models.gd`, `class_name Models`, static)
- `build_gun(weapon_id, skin_id, charm_id := "none") -> Node3D`: the origin is the grip and forward is -Z. Real-world scale. Child `Marker3D`s: `Muzzle`, `Sight` (rear sight, for ADS), and `Support` (off-hand point; absent on the one-handed melee weapons), plus a swinging `GunCharm` named `Charm` when `charm_id` isn't "none". `build_charm(id)`, `build_attachment(id)` and `build_spray(id)` make shop models.
- `Avatar.play_reload(duration)` / `stop_reload()`: third-person reload pose. Remote players' reloads are driven by the `FLAG_RELOAD` bit in their state flags; a remote shot or knife swing stops the pose at once (`Player.remote_fired()`), since firing interrupts a shotgun reload and shots arrive ahead of the interpolated flags. The first-person viewmodel (`scripts/game/viewmodel.gd`) animates the off hand fetching a magazine prop (`Viewmodel.MAGS`) via `ViewmodelArms.set_left_hand()`.
- `build_avatar(outfit_id, hat_id) -> Avatar`. `Avatar` (`class_name Avatar`, `extends Node3D`) has its feet at its origin, faces -Z, and is 1.8 m tall with eyes at 1.6 m. API: `set_look_pitch(rad)`, `set_motion(sliding, lean, local_velocity, grappling)` then `animate(delta, speed: float, on_floor: bool, crouching: bool, prone: bool)` every frame, `hold_weapon(weapon_id, skin_id)` (with `charm_id` set first), `play_shoot()`, `play_slash()`, `play_throw()`, `play_death()` (random fall direction), `reset_pose()`, `set_first_person(enabled)` (shadows only), `flash_hit()`, `set_protected(on)` (spawn-protection shimmer; a hit flash shows over it), `set_tint(material)` (a lasting overlay such as `Models.infected_overlay_material()`; null clears it; hit flash and shimmer show over it), `play_emote(id)` / `stop_emote()` / `pose_emote(id, t)` (win emotes; while one plays it overrides every other pose and the gun is hidden). Outfits (`Data.OUTFITS`: skin, shirt, pants, shoes and accent colours plus a `pattern` style) are painted texel by texel into the avatar atlas (`AVATAR_PARTS` boxes) by `Models._paint` → `_paint_head` / `_paint_torso` / `_paint_arm` / `_paint_thigh` / `_paint_shin`, one `match` case per style (texels with alpha < 1 become emissive, e.g. the robot's lights and the mummy's eyes; `_BOOT_TOP` sets the boot height, `_HAIR` / `_IRIS` the plain faces' colours); `_extra_mesh(outfit_id, mount)` adds 3D details (astronaut and diver backpacks, hazmat mask, robot antenna, knight pauldrons, ninja headband tails). One cached material per outfit (`outfit_material`), shared by avatars and the viewmodel arms. An outfit is only an id in `peer_info.outfit`, so new outfits need no wire change.
- `build_hat(hat_id) -> Node3D`, `build_pickup(kind) -> Node3D`, `build_grenade() -> Node3D`, `build_tactical(kind) -> Node3D`, `build_rocket() -> Node3D`, `skin_material(skin_id) -> Material`. The viewmodel's depth-squashing shader copies a skin's emission texture and operator, so glowing skins look the same in first person as in the shop. Animated skins (`Data.SKINS` entries with `pattern: "anim"` and `anim`: `flow`, `pulse`, `aurora`, `glitch`; Lava Flow, Neon Pulse, Aurora, Glitch) are a `ShaderMaterial` of `Models.ANIM_SKIN_SHADER` instead of a `StandardMaterial3D`: a 32x32 base texture and a mask texture drawn per effect (`_anim_skin_material`), animated on the GPU from `TIME` (nothing per frame on the CPU), with the skin's `a` / `b` colours and `glow` as the emission energy. `anim_skin_shader(viewmodel)` returns the shared shader or its first-person variant (`#define VIEWMODEL` adds the same depth-squashing vertex stage); `Viewmodel._converted_material` swaps an animated skin's material for that variant with every shader parameter (textures, colours, emission energy) copied, so the effect looks the same in first person and never clips into walls. Skins are only ids in `peer_info.skin`, so new skins need no wire change.
- **The Dev Pack** (`DevPack`, `scripts/models/dev_pack.gd`, static; integration notes and previews in `dev_pack/`, which has a `.gdignore`): four code-only cosmetics, `dev_suit` (outfit), `dev_circuit` (skin), `dev_halo` (hat), `dev_core` (charm), listed in `DevPack.IDS` and in the `Data` catalogs with `price: -1` (`Profile.buy` refuses it, `Profile.is_owned` never counts it free) and `dev: true` (the shop's CODE ONLY card, `UIKit.is_dev_item`, and `World._bot_pick` leaving them out of bot loadouts). `Models.outfit_material` (returns `Material`), `skin_material`, `build_hat` and `GunCharm.mesh_for` hand these ids to `DevPack.outfit_material()`, `skin_material()`, `build_hat()` and `charm_mesh(LENGTH)`. Every pack material is a shared, cached `ShaderMaterial` on one of three shaders (`DevPack.shader("outfit" | "skin" | "solid")`), animated on the GPU from `TIME`. The suit's face (two X eyes, a wide stitched smile) isn't in the atlas: `OUTFIT_SHADER` draws it as anti-aliased lines from signed distances (`face_sdf`) inside the head's front-face UV rect (`face_rect`), so it stays smooth at any distance; its mask texture holds static neon (r), circuit traces (g), the head-to-toe pulse phase (b) and the beating chest core (a). Each shader has a `#define VIEWMODEL` variant with the viewmodel depth squash: `Viewmodel._converted_material` routes pack materials to `DevPack.viewmodel_material(m)`, so they keep their animation in first person. **Kill flash:** `World._s_killed` (every peer) calls `DevPack.flash(killer)` and, for the local killer, `DevPack.flash(local_player.viewmodel)`; `flash()` tweens the `instance uniform float kill_flash` per `GeometryInstance3D` from 1 to 0 over `FLASH_TIME` (0.7 s), so the materials stay shared and only the killer's pieces light up (instance uniforms need Forward+ or Mobile, not Compatibility); anything not from the pack is skipped. **Unlock:** `Profile.redeem_code(code)` checks `DevPack.code_matches` (SHA-256 of the lower-cased code without spaces against `UNLOCK_CODE_SHA256`; the plain code never ships) and adds the four ids to `owned`, returning `REDEEM_UNLOCKED`, `REDEEM_ALREADY` or `REDEEM_INVALID`. Its only caller is `CreditsPanel` (`ui/credits_panel.gd`): Stats > CREDITS opens it as an overlay (the page behind gets `focus_behavior_recursive = DISABLED`, so pad focus stays inside); the DEV row opens a keypad of 36 rounded keys plus CLEAR / BACKSPACE / ENTER, and its `_input` takes keyboard letters, digits, Backspace, Delete and Enter, swallows Space, and steps back on `ui_cancel`. The shop is display-only for the pack (`DevCardButton`, `DevBadgePanel`; no Buy button), the locker's picker shows a selected pack piece with `DevOptionButton`. `Net.sanitize_info` already accepts any catalog id, so the pack travels in `peer_info` like any cosmetic.

### World (`scripts/game/world.gd`) and Player additions for the modes
- `World.rules: MatchMode` (always set), `World.tactical: Tactical` (the same node in Tactical, else null), `World.streaks: Streaks` (always set; idle when killstreaks are off), `World.pings: Pings` (always set; idle outside team modes), `World.sprays: Sprays` (always set), `World.spectator: Spectator` and `World.photo: PhotoMode` (always set), `request_spectate(on)` (this player spectates or plays: `Net.set_spectating`), `attacks_blocked() -> bool` (`rules.attacks_blocked()`), `is_enemy(a: Player, b: Player) -> bool` (anyone else except a teammate), `ready_peer_ids() -> Array` (peers that loaded the world, for modules sending their own RPCs), `pick_spawn(for_id, team := -1) -> [pos, yaw]` (the standard spawn pick, away from players not on `team`), `server_blast(pos, radius, max_dmg, owner_id, wid, self_scale := 0.5)` (server: explosion damage with falloff and wall cover, used by rockets, frags and the Block Bomber). `Player.shield` is the Overshield (every peer, from the server; 0 after death and respawn). `World.local_player` is null for a spectator. `Hud.small_message(text, good := true)` shows a short line where pickups are announced.
- Server helpers the mode controllers use: `server_add_bot(team) -> int`, `server_add_human(pid, team, is_alive)`, `server_remove_bot(eid)`, `server_remove_entity(eid)`, `server_place(eid, pos, yaw)` (teleport, like a respawn), `server_set_health(eid, hp)`, `server_end_match()`, `server_balance_bots()` (the standard bot balance).
- `server_add_bot(team, info := {})` takes the bot's info instead of a random one (FiringRange's dummies); `refill_equipment(eid)` gives `eid` its frags and tactical again on the server's count and the player's (offline practice). `Player.top_up_reserves()` refills every gun's reserve, magazines kept. A bot whose info has `dummy` gets no `BotBrain`.
- `Player.slots` entries may be `""` (an empty slot: Tactical before a pickup, Infected always, Gun Game's unused slot): switching, ammo and the HUD skip them, and the held slot is always armed (the knife slot always is). `Player.give_weapon(slot, wid)` puts a gun in slot 0 (primary) or 1 (secondary), or a melee weapon in slot 2, with a fresh magazine and reserve, updates `info`, and draws it when the player held their melee weapon or the replaced weapon. Slot 2 always holds a melee weapon (`Player.slots_from(info)`: `info.melee` if `Data.is_melee`, else the knife); `Player.melee_id()` is what the quick melee swings.
- Role fields in `Player.info` (server-set, sent with the entity): `team`, `max_hp` (`Player.max_health()`, default `Data.MAX_HEALTH`; spawn, respawn, regen, health crates and Vampire fill to it), `speed` (multiplies `Player.speed_scale`), `knife_only` (Infected: no grenades or tactical), `no_grenades` (Gun Game: no frags or tactical; static `Player.carries_grenades(info)` checks both), `ladder` (Gun Game step). `Player.apply_info(info)` applies a mid-match change on every peer (slots, ammo, equipment, speed; a reload or burst of the old gun ends).

### Spectator (`scripts/game/spectator.gd`, `class_name Spectator extends Node`; World's child "Spectator", `World.spectator`)
- `enum View { OFF, FREE, FOLLOW }`, `view`, `target: Player`, `DEATH_HOLD` (2 s on a followed player who died before the next). It starts by itself when `is_spectator()` (`Net.is_spectator(me)` and no local player) and stops when that ends; `begin_dead()` starts the follow-only dead mode (Tactical). `is_active()`, `is_dead_follow()`, `camera()`.
- FOLLOW: the FreeCam at `target.head` with the target's yaw, pitch, lean roll and sights FOV; the target's avatar `set_first_person(true)`, nametag hidden; a `Viewmodel` built from the target's info (rebuilt only when the look changes) posed from `net_flags()` (weapon, aim, sprint, slide, crouch, reload, floor) and the measured velocity. World calls `on_shot(p, wid)` (`_play_shot_fx`: kick or knife swing) and `on_launch(owner_id, kind)` (`_s_spawn_projectile`: rocket kick or throw). The target's Laser Sight beam leaves this viewmodel's muzzle (`Player.laser_viewmodel`, cleared on release). Candidates: `World.rules.spectate_candidates()`. A spectator who has nobody to follow yet flies FREE until someone appears.
- Input (`_unhandled_input`, not while the Hud blocks input): `fire` / `next_weapon` next, `aim` / `prev_weapon` previous (FREE: `fire` follows the player nearest the middle of the view), `spectate_view` FREE <-> FOLLOW (not in dead mode); mouse look in FREE. `set_photo(on)` (PhotoMode), `watched() -> {view, dead_mode, id, name, team, alive, health, max_hp, shield, weapon, score}`, `score_line(eid)`.
- UI: `SpectatorOverlay` (`ui/spectator_overlay.gd`, canvas layer 11): the bottom banner (SPECTATING + name or FREE CAMERA, key hints; hidden in dead mode, where Tactical's bar names the teammate) and the followed player's card at the bottom right (name, health bar with shield, weapon, score).
